# Knowledge base outline

Northwind Auto is a **fictional** used-car retailer that also buys cars from the public. Its help center is the knowledge base (KB) for this cookbook. Most articles are for people **selling** a car to Northwind; a few are for people who **bought** from Northwind and are there because they look alike. The company, its states and its policies are invented for illustration and are not legal information.

This outline lists what the KB covers, without the article text, so that test questions can be written in a customer's own words before the articles exist.

## Versions

The help center was rewritten on **2026-02-01**.

- **Changed articles** have two versions. The **2022** version was in effect from 2022-01-01 to 2026-01-31 and is now *superseded*. The **2026** version is in effect from 2026-02-01 and is *current*.
- **Unchanged articles** have one version, effective since 2022-01-01. They are still current.
- So *stale* means superseded, not old. A 2022 article that was never rewritten is still the right answer.

## States (fictional)
| State | What is different |
|---|---|
| Aldora | Electronic titles; duplicate titles online in about a week |
| Brevin | Paper titles; duplicates take 4–6 weeks; cars from other states need an emissions certificate |
| Corlan | Paper titles; a power of attorney must be notarized, even NW-POA-2 |
